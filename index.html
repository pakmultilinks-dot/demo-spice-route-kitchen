Our story

A family table with Lahore at heart.

Spice Route Kitchen is imagined as a neighbourhood desi restaurant built around one simple idea: the best meals arrive hot, generous and ready to be shared.

Our fictional kitchen borrows from family recipes across Punjab—smoky charcoal grills, hand-ground masalas and karahis finished to order. The menu keeps familiar favourites close while giving every plate a bright, contemporary finish.

Whether it is a quick biryani lunch or a full family dinner, the welcome stays warm and the naan keeps coming.
